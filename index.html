ShadowFill

How wrong are passive-fill backtests? Measured against a ground truth computed by exact queue arithmetic on Nasdaq market-by-order data — not modelled.

Headline A passive order resting at AAPL's touch fills 43% of the time within a minute; Kaplan–Meier on the same book says 16%. Every bootstrap interval excludes zero.

A live order-flow terminal for Binance USDT-M perpetuals: computes OFI on every quote change and shows in real time how much of each price move order flow explains.
